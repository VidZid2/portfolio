# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project and community a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

As a 2nd-year BSIT student building open-source web projects and UI systems, I believe in creating a supportive learning environment where developers of all skill levels can collaborate, ask questions, give feedback, and build together with mutual respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward fellow developers and learners
* Being respectful of differing opinions, design viewpoints, and technical experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Taking responsibility, acknowledging mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best for the project and the broader developer community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment of any kind
* Publishing others' private information, such as email or contact details, without explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative developer environment

## Enforcement Responsibilities

As the maintainer of this project, I am responsible for clarifying and upholding these standards of acceptable behavior. I will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed harmful, offensive, or counter to these values.

The project maintainer reserves the right to remove, edit, or reject issues, pull requests, comments, commits, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, discussions, pull requests, and any public spaces where an individual represents this project.
